# Coat Colours and Markings

Common colours: golden, black, tricolour, grey tabby, orange tabby, cream, white, brown.
Common markings: white chest patch, white blaze, white paw, striped tail, dark points, floppy ears, scarred ear, green eyes, blue eyes.
Record markings exactly as you see them — they are the strongest way to tell two similar-looking pets apart when breed and colour match.
