# Lost-Pet Search Guidance (curated summary)

## First 24 hours
Search the last-seen area on foot, at dusk and dawn when cats and dogs move. Call the pet's name, bring a familiar smell (bedding, your clothing). Tell neighbours and local shelters the same day.

## What helps a match
Species, breed, colour, size, sex, and distinctive markings (white chest patch, striped tail, scarred ear) matter more than a photo alone. Note the date and the exact last-seen location, and keep vet records and photos ready to verify identity in person.

## Safety
Never share your home address or phone number publicly. Meet in a public place, bring a friend, and verify with photos and vet records before handing a pet over. If a pet looks injured, contact a vet — do not try to treat it yourself.
