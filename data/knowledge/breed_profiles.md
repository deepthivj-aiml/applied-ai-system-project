# Breed Profiles (curated summary, for retrieval only)

## Golden Retriever
Large dog, golden coat, friendly, high energy. Needs 60-90 minutes of activity a day. Distinctive: feathered coat, white chest patch is common in some lines. Prone to wandering when off-lead near water.

## Beagle
Medium dog, tricolour coat, white blaze on the face, black saddle. Scent-driven: follows smells and can travel far quickly. Loud baying bark.

## Labrador
Large dog, black, chocolate or yellow coat. Friendly, food-motivated, strong swimmer. A white paw or chest patch helps tell individuals apart.

## Tabby Cat
Not a breed but a coat pattern: striped tail, grey or orange tabby markings. Cats hide when lost; search at dusk, within a few hundred metres of home, in garages and under porches.

## Siamese Cat
Small cat, cream coat with dark points on ears, face, paws and tail, blue eyes. Very vocal. Stays close to home when lost but hides well.
